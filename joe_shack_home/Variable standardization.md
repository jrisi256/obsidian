$$
\frac{X - \large{\mu_x}}{\large{\sigma_x}}
$$

* $X$ is a random variable.
* $\mu_x$ is the mean of $X$.
* $\sigma_x$ is the standard deviation of $X$.

Standardizing a variable transforms the variable so it has a mean of 0 and a standard deviation of 1. It has a wide variety of applications. Usually it is done, to place variables on the same scale allowing for a more apples-to-apples comparison. Some modeling algorithms will not work correctly unless the variables are standardized. It is also commonly done in regression analyses to allow for an easier comparison of the effect sizes of the variables when comparing the coefficients although it is not a perfect method for doing so.
